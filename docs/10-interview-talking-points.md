# 10 - Interview Talking Points

## 30-Second Summary
> I worked on a cybersecurity risk assessment for a fictional e-commerce environment. We identified crown-jewel assets, developed risk scenarios, assessed likelihood and impact, mapped preventive/detective/corrective controls, documented residual risk, and connected treatment priorities to cost and executive decision-making. When I turned it into a portfolio project, I also documented weaknesses in the original scoring approach instead of overstating its precision.

## Be Ready to Explain
- Inherent vs. residual risk
- Preventive vs. detective vs. corrective controls
- Control coverage vs. control effectiveness
- Risk appetite and why it matters
- Why a formal scoring matrix matters
- Why inconsistent source data should be flagged
- How cyber risk becomes a funding and management decision
