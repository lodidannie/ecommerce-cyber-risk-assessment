# 07 - Cost-Benefit Analysis

| Risk Area | Total Risk | Residual Risk | Risk Reduction | Control Cost | Reported ROI |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Data Breach | $350,000 | $105,000 | $245,000 | $60,000 | 308% |
| Application Exploits | $280,000 | $70,000 | $210,000 | $40,000 | 425% |
| Misuse / Non-Compliance | $20,000 | $10,000 | $10,000 | $15,000 | -33% |

The figures are retained as source-provided scenario estimates. The source does not document a complete quantitative loss model for the starting exposure values.

For production use I would document asset-value basis, event frequency, loss magnitude, control-effectiveness assumptions, uncertainty/ranges and time horizon before relying on ROI.
