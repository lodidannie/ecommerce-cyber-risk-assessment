# 01 - Project Scope

## Objective
Assess cyber risk to a fictional e-commerce platform and translate technical exposure into prioritized security and business actions.

## In Scope
Customer/payment information; e-commerce application and APIs; databases/backend logic; order, logistics, and inventory systems; intellectual property/vendor data; customer trust and brand reputation.

## Key Questions
1. What assets matter most?
2. What could go wrong?
3. What likelihood and impact were assigned?
4. What controls reduce exposure?
5. What risk remains?
6. What should management prioritize?
7. What does treatment cost?

## Boundary
The source does not include a complete architecture, control-test evidence, threat-intelligence dataset, or historical incident dataset. This is a scenario-based assessment, not an audit or penetration test.
