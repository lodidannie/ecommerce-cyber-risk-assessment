# 04 - Risk Analysis

## R-01 Data Breach
Medium likelihood, High impact, Medium inherent rating, Medium residual rating. Controls include encryption, Zero Trust, MFA/access controls, DLP, IDS, SIEM, backups and incident response.

## R-02 Application Exploitation
Medium likelihood, High impact, High inherent rating, Medium residual rating. Scenarios include SQL injection, XSS and API abuse; residual concerns include zero-days, supply-chain issues, misconfiguration and privilege escalation.

## R-03 Misuse / Non-Compliance
Low likelihood, Medium impact, Low inherent and residual ratings. The source emphasizes policy, awareness, least privilege, secure protocols, auditing and alerting.

## R-04 Insider Threat
Low likelihood, High impact, Low inherent and residual ratings. The source lists background checks, RBAC, monitoring, anomaly detection, SIEM, offboarding and incident response.

## R-05 Denial of Service
Medium likelihood, Medium impact, High inherent rating, Medium residual rating. Controls include WAF/IPS, CDN, rate limiting and traffic throttling.
