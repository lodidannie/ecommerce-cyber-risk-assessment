# 03 - Risk Assessment Methodology

## Source Approach
The original assessment records qualitative likelihood, impact, inherent risk, controls, residual-risk scenarios, and residual risk. Controls are grouped as preventive, detective, or corrective.

## Portfolio Process
1. Identify crown jewels.
2. Define risk scenarios.
3. Record source likelihood and impact.
4. Preserve the source inherent-risk rating.
5. Map controls.
6. Identify residual-risk scenarios.
7. Preserve the source residual-risk rating.
8. Recommend treatment and timing.
9. Connect treatment to cost where source estimates exist.
10. Communicate decisions to executives.

## Production Improvement
A real assessment should define likelihood bands, impact thresholds, matrix rules, risk appetite/tolerance, control-effectiveness criteria, accountable owners, acceptance authority, and reassessment cadence.
