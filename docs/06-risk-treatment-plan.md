# 06 - Risk Treatment Plan

| Risk | Inherent | Residual | Treatment Direction | Source Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Application exploitation | High | Medium | Mitigate | Within 3 months |
| Denial of service | High | Medium | Mitigate | Within 3 months |
| Data breach | Medium | Medium | Mitigate | Within 3 months |
| Misuse / non-compliance | Low | Low | Mitigate / monitor | Within 6-12 months |
| Insider threat | Low | Low | Mitigate / monitor | Within 6-12 months |

A production remediation plan should add risk owner, control owner, budget owner, status, target date, evidence, exception process and formal residual-risk acceptance.
